# Playlist arranging

Arrange the songs someone has already chosen into a listening experience they prefer. A better arrangement depends on the listener's intent.

## Language

**Playlist**:
An ordered collection of entries chosen by a listener. The same recording can appear more than once.

**Playlist entry**:
One occurrence of an item in a playlist, including repeated songs, local files, and unavailable items. Two entries can refer to the same recording while occupying different positions.
_Avoid_: Unique song, when referring to something being moved.

**Recording**:
A specific recorded version of a song. Live performances, remixes, radio edits, and studio releases can be different recordings of the same song.
_Avoid_: Song title, as proof that two recordings are the same.

**Recording match**:
An audio source believed to contain the recording represented by a playlist entry. A plausible match can still be wrong.

**Arrangement**:
A proposed order of the existing playlist entries. Arranging preserves every occurrence and does not select, add, or remove songs.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, when only the order changes.

**Transition**:
The listening boundary between the ending of one entry and the beginning of the next. Reversing the entries creates a different transition.

**Listening profile**:
The desired pacing and amount of variation for an arrangement. A profile expresses listening intent rather than a universal measure of musical quality.

**Fixed entry**:
A playlist entry that retains its original position in an arrangement. An entry with unavailable or untrusted analysis can remain fixed without disappearing from the playlist.

**Preview**:
The complete arrangement presented for review before saving. It includes fixed entries and repeated songs in the positions the listener would actually hear.

**Pin**:
A listener's requirement that a particular playlist entry occupy a chosen position, such as the beginning or end.

**Analysis coverage**:
The portion of a playlist's entries or transitions with enough trusted measurements to assess. Coverage does not measure how much a listener will like the arrangement.

**Estimated intensity**:
A recording's relative strength based on its audio level and rhythmic activity compared with the other recordings in the playlist. It is not a mood label or a measurement of emotional energy.

**Listening time**:
The elapsed time in an arrangement when every entry plays in full without overlap. Pauses, skipping and crossfade can change the actual playback time.

**Listening note**:
A short description of a measured change or nearby artist repeat at an actual boundary in an arrangement. It identifies something for the listener to judge by listening.
